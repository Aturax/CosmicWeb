Who's behind it

Hi, I'm Alberto

I'm a programmer and I've been making games for years. I started with mobile games and game jams, and since 2021 I've been doing it professionally. The Cosmic Works games I make on my own, for PC and Steam Deck.

I care about design as much as code, especially figuring out why one mechanic works and another doesn't. The studio's motto is "gaming passion": I play a lot, and that's where most of my ideas come from.

On YouTube I teach how to make video games with Unity, using my own games (it's further down, under Learn). The videos are in Spanish.

FAQ

Where can I learn to make video games?

On my YouTube channel, @cosmicworks. The videos are in Spanish and I almost always use Unity, which you can use for free.

What do I need to start making games?

A computer and an engine like Unity or Godot, which are free. I'd start with a very short game or by joining a game jam, not with the big game you have in your head.

What is an indie developer?

Someone who makes games without a publisher behind them. Cosmic Works is my studio. I design and program the games in Ciudad Real, Spain, and work with other people on art and music.

Which engine and tools do I use?

Unity 6 with C# and .NET, and Steamworks to publish on Steam. My games come out for PC (Windows, macOS and Linux) and Steam Deck.

Is there a Spanish-speaking indie dev community?

Yes. From 2020 to 2023 we ran the Reto GameDev, a team game jam for people making games in Spanish.

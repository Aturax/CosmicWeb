Tiki Tiki: The Tropical Memory Game

A memory game played with tiki totems on a tropical beach. It first came out in 2020 and comes back to Steam on 1 December 2026 with a new look and a fourth game.

Modes

Four memory games

Everything is done with carved wooden tikis stacked into totems.

Twin Peeks

You get a few seconds to look at a board of cards before they flip over, then you find the pairs.

Twist of Face

The tikis turn left or right, one after another, and you repeat the turns in the same order.

Tiki Says

The tikis light up and play a note each, Simon-style, and you repeat the sequence.

New

Totem Recall

The game shows you, one at a time, which face each tiki has to end up showing. Then you turn them into place from memory and confirm before the time runs out.

How it plays

24 ways to play

Each game has three sizes and two rules. In Challenge there's no clock. You have three lives and a goal to reach, and a long enough streak gives you a life back. In Time Attack the clock only counts down while it's your turn, and you get a bit of time back for every hit.

Each of the 24 setups keeps its own best score and its own bronze, silver and gold medal. On top of that there are 24 Tiki Trials (small one-off feats) and 23 Steam achievements.

The story

Why it's back

I released Tiki Tiki on Steam on 28 August 2020. I sold it as a brain-training game for families, which was probably a mistake. The ten people who reviewed it liked it, but very few found it in the first place, and I left it alone for years.

This year, before starting anything new, I went back to it. I moved it to Unity 6 and redrew every menu and button so they look carved from the same wood as the totems. The three original games got new names and better rules, and I added Totem Recall. You can now play the whole thing with a controller, on Windows, macOS, Linux or a Steam Deck. If you bought it in 2020, you get the update for free.

Alberto Blanco

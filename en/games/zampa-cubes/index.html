Zampa Cubes

It was the first Cosmic Works game, released for mobile in 2016. Ten years later I've given it another go with new modes, and now you can play it in your browser with keyboard, gamepad or touch.

Play here See it on itch.io ↗

Play

Play now

Press to load the game. Then tap the screen on mobile, or click on a computer, and choose a mode.

Modes

Three ways to play

Classic Mode

Collect cubes and cross an edge to move to the next face of the cube.

Time Attack

Every point buys you more time.

Versus Mode

Challenge someone on the same computer. One player uses WASD and the other the arrow keys.

How it plays

A cube with six faces

You move the white ball across one face of the cube, collect the cubes and dodge the black balls. Reach the edge and you jump to another face. Each level is faster than the last.
